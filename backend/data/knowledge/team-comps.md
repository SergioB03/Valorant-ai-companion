# Team Compositions — Archetypes and Map Fits

Comp thinking that survives patches, plus late-2025 flavor examples. Agent
tiers move, so treat the specific names as examples of the archetype.

## Comp building principles

- Non-negotiable: at least one controller. Comps without smokes lose to
  ordinary crossfires regardless of aim.
- The standard skeleton is duelist + initiator + controller + sentinel + flex;
  the flex slot is what map-tuning actually changes.
- Utility must chain: recon that finds the anchor, flash that breaks the
  angle, duelist who swings it, smokes that cut the crossfire, sentinel who
  keeps the flank honest. If your comp can't run that chain, rounds decay
  into pure aim duels.
- In solo queue, prefer self-sufficient versions of each role (Omen over
  Astra, Reyna/Jett over pure team-dependent entry) because coordination is
  scarce.

## Archetype: double initiator

- Shape: 1 duelist, 2 initiators, 1 controller, 1 sentinel.
- Why: maximizes utility for methodical executes and retakes; the default
  shape of coordinated/pro play on most maps through 2024–2025.
- Example flavor (late 2025): Jett, Sova + Gekko (or Tejo), Omen, Cypher on
  Ascent-like maps.
- Weakness: slightly light on raw entry; needs the initiators to actually
  follow and trade.

## Archetype: double controller

- Shape: 1 duelist, 1 initiator, 2 controllers, 1 sentinel (or 2nd initiator
  instead of the sentinel).
- Why: maps with huge sightlines (Icebox, Breeze, Abyss) need more vision
  denial than one agent can supply — classically Viper plus a second smoke.
- Weakness: slower tempo; can struggle to punish fast hits without a second
  info agent.

## Archetype: double duelist

- Shape: 2 duelists, 1 initiator, 1 controller, 1 sentinel.
- Why: tight, short-rotation maps (Split, Bind, Sunset) reward repeated fast
  hits where the second duelist trades the first instantly.
- Weakness: utility-poor; falls apart if both duelists play for their own
  highlight instead of sequencing swings.

## Archetype: no sentinel (fast tempo)

- Shape: 1–2 duelists, 2 initiators, 1 controller.
- Why: all-in on tempo — hit fast before flanks matter, retake with utility
  instead of anchoring. Seen on Fracture and attack-sided maps.
- Weakness: flank control and post-plant lockdown suffer; punishable by
  patient lurkers.

## Map-fit rules of thumb (late-2025 flavor)

- Icebox / Breeze: Viper effectively required; add Sova for open-space recon.
- Bind: Raze and Brimstone shine (satchel space-taking, molly post-plants);
  teleporters reward Cypher flank intel.
- Haven / Lotus (3 sites): info agents and fast-rotating smokes (Omen) rise in
  value; sentinels who can hold a site 1vX (Killjoy) anchor the weak site.
- Ascent: the classic Killjoy B / Omen / Sova core plus Jett Op has been meta
  for years.
- Fracture: Breach and Brimstone historically excellent; double-sided hits
  want stuns over flashes.
- Corrode (newest, June 2025): meta was still settling at this snapshot —
  standard Omen/Cypher core with initiator-heavy clears was the early default.

## Ranked-specific comp advice

- Don't fight instalockers — fill the highest-impact missing slot, which is
  almost always smokes.
- A comp with 4 duelists and your smokes still wins if the duelists get
  traded; call the simple plan ("I smoke, you go") rather than complaining.
- Below Ascendant, comp composition matters far less than trading discipline
  and economy sync. Pick agents you can pilot, not agents a tier list likes.
- If you queue with a duo, pair complementary roles (initiator + duelist,
  controller + sentinel) and always buy together.
