Full context and other dev notes about the updates may be found in the official notes.

## Patch 13.04 — Map Updates
### Map Rotation
- Breeze rotated out.
- Abyss rotated in.

## Patch 13.04 — Agent Updates
### Cypher
- Spycam
  - Bugfix: Minimap icon was rotated incorrectly.

### Viper
- Snake Bite
  - Bugfix: Equip animation appeared incorrectly from an enemy's point of view

### Deadlock
- GravNet
  - Bugfix: Visual effects could disappear after a player disconnected and reconnected.

### Clove
- Meddle and Ruse
  - Bugfix: Repeatedly switching between Meddle and Ruse could cause a player to freeze and disconnect

## Patch 13.04 — Modes Updates
### Premier
- Stage V26A5 will run for five weeks instead of the usual seven
- Teams with a Premier Score of at least 450 qualify for Playoffs on September 20.
  - In Contender and Invite, qualification is still based on your placement

## Patch 13.04 — Cosmetic Updates
- PC ONLY Bugfix: Animation on all transforming melee weapons (like Kuronami 2.0 and Nocturnum) would not transform during their inspect.
