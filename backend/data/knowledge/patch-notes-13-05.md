The following is a list of patch v13.05's changes and updates to VALORANT.

Full context and other dev notes about the updates may be found in the official notes.

## Patch 13.05 — Agent Updates
### General
- Bugfix: Ultimate VO could continue playing from an enemy's perspective after the Agent casting was killed.
- Bugfix: Combat Report incorrectly displayed "Enemies Blinded" for certain abilities that don't apply Blind.
- Bugfix: Some smokes blocking flashes and other LOS-dependent abilities after dissipating.
  - Affected Agents: Astra's Nebula/Dissipate, Brimstone's Sky Smoke, Clove's Ruse, Miks's Waveform, and Omen's Dark Cover

### Chamber
- Headhunter
  - Bugfix: Could cause performance drops the first time he equipped, aimed, or fired it during a round.

### Cypher
- Trapwire
  - Bugfix: Debuff persists indefinitely on a new target if the initial target was killed just before it triggered
- Cyber Cage
  - Bugfix: Incorrectly blocking flashes, detection, and other line-of-sight-dependent abilities

### Deadlock
- GravNet
  - Bugfix: Could briefly display unintended VFX on screen.

### KAYO
- NULL/cmd
  - Bugfix: Causing client-side framerate drops with each pulse.

### Omen
- Dark Cover
  - Bugfix: Could obscure a player’s vision after the smoke has dissipated.

### Sage
- Healing Orb
  - Bugfix: Ability tooltips could be missing from the HUD when equipping the ability.

### Waylay
- Saturate
  - Bugfix: Vision-blocking abilities could prevent it from applying Hinder to players within its area of effect.
  - Bugfix: Could apply Hinder to Agents while they were intangible.
- Refract
  - Bugfix: Could cause snapshots of dynamic map objects to remain visible after the ability ended.

## Patch 13.05 — Map Updates
### Lotus
- Bugfix: Fade’s Seize not displaying its danger indicator to enemies in certain locations.

### The Range
These changes apply to PC only.
- Options for default bot count have been added: 10, 5, or 1 to spawn.
- The default number of bots spawned is now 5.
