# Recent Patch Summaries (2025 season)

IMPORTANT: These are condensed summaries reconstructed from training data
(cutoff early 2026). Version numbers and dates are approximate, individual
numbers may be off, and anything after early 2026 is missing. For exact
values always check the official patch notes at playvalorant.com.

## Patch 10.00 — early January 2025 (summary)

- Season 2025 begins: Riot reorganized the competitive calendar into the new
  yearly season structure with shorter Acts.
- **Tejo released** — a new initiator with targetable missile strikes (Guided
  Salvo), a stun drone, a stealth recon drone, and a large area-denial ultimate.
  He immediately became one of the strongest utility-clear agents in the game.
- Map pool rotation for the new season and standard start-of-year systems
  updates (battlepass, ranked act rollover).

## Patch 10.04 — March 2025 (summary)

- **Waylay released** — a radiant-light duelist. Kit highlights: a dash with
  flexible trajectory, a light-based blind (Saturate), a bounce/refract mobility
  tool, and an ultimate that projects a zone slowing enemies caught inside.
- Balance tuning around the new agent's arrival plus quality-of-life fixes.
- Waylay settled in as a reasonable but not oppressive entry duelist.

## Patches 10.05–10.06 — spring 2025 (summary)

- **Tejo nerfs**: after Tejo dominated early VCT 2025 play (extremely high pick
  rates), Riot reduced the power and availability of Guided Salvo and trimmed
  his utility uptime. He remained viable but no longer auto-locked.
- Assorted duelist and sentinel tuning aimed at diversifying pro-play comps.

## Patch ~11.00 — June 2025 (summary)

- Mid-season Act start.
- **Corrode released** — a new two-site map added to the competitive rotation,
  with the map pool rotating accordingly.
- Standard new-Act ranked squish and battlepass rollover.
- Continued balance passes on the early-2025 agent releases.

## Patches 11.0x — mid-to-late 2025 (summary)

- Iterative balance throughout the second half of 2025: controller and
  sentinel tuning, continued adjustments to Tejo and Waylay, and periodic
  weapon-price/economy microadjustments.
- Esports-driven changes ahead of Champions 2025 (Paris) to stabilize the
  competitive meta.

## Late 2025 — new sentinel (summary, low confidence)

- A new sentinel, **Veto**, was revealed around Champions 2025 and released in
  the fall. Positioned as a brute-force, anti-utility style sentinel. Details
  in training data are thin — treat this entry as a pointer, not a source of
  truth, and confirm his kit from official notes.

## How to use these summaries

- Use them for direction ("Tejo was strong then nerfed through 2025", "Corrode
  was 2025's newest map") rather than exact numbers.
- These reconstructed summaries end in late 2025. Newer, real patch notes live
  in the per-patch files alongside this one (patch-notes-13-04.md and later,
  ingested from the community wiki) — prefer those for anything recent.
- Agent win rates today and unreleased content are outside the knowledge base
  entirely — say so when asked.
- Patch cadence is roughly every two weeks, with larger changes at Act
  boundaries.
