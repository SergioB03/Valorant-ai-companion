# Valorant Agents — Roles, Kits and Meta Sentiment

This file summarizes the agent roster and general meta sentiment as of late 2025 /
early 2026 (training data). Balance shifts every patch, so tier opinions here are
directional, not gospel.

## Duelists

Duelists are the primary space-takers. Their kits are built around self-sufficient
entry: dashes, flashes, and self-heals that let them take the first fight on a site.

- **Jett** — Tailwind dash, Updraft, Cloudburst smokes, Blade Storm ultimate. The
  classic Operator agent because dash lets her peek and reposition safely. Perennially
  strong; repeatedly nerfed (dash now requires activation) but still a top pro pick.
- **Raze** — Blast Pack movement, Paint Shells grenade, Boom Bot, Showstopper rocket.
  Highest raw damage output of any duelist. Dominant on tight maps like Split, Bind
  and Fracture where satchel movement and nade damage shine.
- **Reyna** — Leer (nearsight orb), Devour heal and Dismiss intangibility on kills,
  Empress ultimate. Snowballs hard off kills, which makes her a ranked queue monster
  and a rare pro pick. Reworked so Devour/Dismiss charges are more forgiving.
- **Phoenix** — Curveball flash, Hot Hands molly/self-heal, Blaze wall, Run It Back.
  Solid beginner duelist; buffs over the years keep him playable but rarely meta.
- **Yoru** — Gatecrash teleport, Blindside flash, Fakeout clone, Dimensional Drift.
  High skill ceiling, lurk-heavy playstyle. Niche but sees pockets of pro play.
- **Neon** — High Gear sprint/slide, Relay Bolt stun, Fast Lane walls, Overdrive.
  Movement-shooting duelist. Her slide accuracy and charge count have been nerfed
  and re-tuned multiple times; strong on long maps when slide is in a good state.
- **Iso** — Double Tap shield (gained an automatic shield in his 8.11 rework),
  Undercut wall-piercing debuff, Contingency wall, Kill Contract 1v1 arena. Was very
  strong after the rework, then trimmed back; sits mid-tier.
- **Waylay** — Released March 2025 (patch ~10.04). A radiant-light duelist with a
  dash that can be redirected, a light-based blind (Saturate), and an ultimate that
  slows enemies in a large zone. Landed as a decent but not oppressive entry option.

## Initiators

Initiators gather info and break defensive setups so the team can execute. In
coordinated play this is usually the highest-impact role.

- **Sova** — Recon Bolt, Shock Bolt, Owl Drone, Hunter's Fury. The info king on open
  maps (Ascent, Icebox, Breeze). Lineup knowledge dramatically raises his ceiling.
- **Breach** — Flashpoint, Fault Line stun, Aftershock, Rolling Thunder. Excellent on
  maps with lots of walls to flash through (Fracture, Split, Lotus).
- **Skye** — Guiding Light bird flash, Trailblazer dog, Regrowth heal, Seekers.
  Was a must-pick until her flash and heal were heavily nerfed (2024); now mid-tier.
- **KAY/O** — FLASH/drive, ZERO/point suppression knife, FRAG/ment, NULL/cmd.
  Suppression counters ability-reliant comps; consistently strong in pro play.
- **Fade** — Haunt reveal, Seize, Prowlers, Nightfall. Strong Sova alternative on
  tighter maps; Prowlers clear corners that recon darts cannot.
- **Gekko** — Wingman (can plant/defuse), Dizzy flash, Mosh Pit, Thrash. Reclaimable
  ability creatures give great round economy; a staple initiator since release.
- **Tejo** — Released January 2025 (patch ~10.00). Missile-based initiator: Guided
  Salvo (targetable missile strikes), Special Delivery stun drone, Stealth Drone,
  and a large area-denial ultimate. Dominated the early 2025 pro meta and was
  nerfed multiple times during the year; still a strong utility clear agent.

## Controllers

Controllers own vision denial. Every serious comp runs at least one.

- **Omen** — Dark Cover smokes (rechargeable), Paranoia blind, Shrouded Step TP,
  From the Shadows. The most flexible and most-picked controller overall.
- **Brimstone** — Sky Smokes, Incendiary, Stim Beacon, Orbital Strike. Simple and
  effective; strong on maps with tight post-plant molly lineups (Bind, Fracture).
- **Viper** — Toxic Screen wall, Poison Cloud, Snake Bite, Viper's Pit. Map-specific
  powerhouse (Icebox, Breeze). Utility uptime nerfs pushed her to one-smoker-plus
  status on most other maps.
- **Astra** — Global star placement: smokes, Gravity Well, Nova Pulse, Cosmic Divide.
  High coordination requirement; appears mostly in organized team play.
- **Harbor** — Water-based walls and covers (High Tide, Cascade, Cove), Reckoning.
  Usually a second controller in double-controller comps; niche.
- **Clove** — Smokes castable even after death, Meddle decay, Pick-Me-Up, Not Dead
  Yet self-revive ultimate. Extremely popular in ranked because death doesn't stop
  their smoke duty; solid but not oppressive in pro play.

## Sentinels

Sentinels lock down flanks and anchor sites with traps and objects.

- **Killjoy** — Turret, Alarmbot, Nanoswarms, Lockdown. Premier site anchor on maps
  with defensible corners (Ascent, Lotus).
- **Cypher** — Trapwires, Spycam, Cyber Cage, Neural Theft. Trip buffs made him a
  top-tier info sentinel through 2024–2025; great for catching flankers.
- **Sage** — Barrier Orb wall, Slow Orbs, Healing Orb, Resurrection. The only true
  healer; wall creates plant/boost angles. Always decent in ranked.
- **Chamber** — Headhunter pistol, Rendezvous TP, Trademark trap, Tour De Force.
  Was game-warpingly strong in 2022, nerfed into a niche pick; occasionally
  resurfaces in pro play on maps that favor his TP angles.
- **Deadlock** — GravNet, Sonic Sensor, Barrier Mesh, Annihilation. Received several
  buffs since release; situational, best against rush-heavy comps.
- **Vyse** — Released August 2024 (patch ~9.04). Liquid-metal sentinel: Shear hidden
  wall, Arc Rose flash, Razorvine, Steel Garden (jams primary weapons). Settled in
  as a solid, flexible sentinel option.
- **Veto** — Sentinel released late 2025. Anti-utility bruiser concept revealed
  around Champions 2025. Kit details in this file may be incomplete or outdated —
  verify with current patch notes.

## General meta sentiment (late 2025 snapshot)

- Most contested picks: Omen, Cypher, Gekko, Tejo (post-nerfs still relevant),
  Jett and Raze splitting the entry-duelist slot by map.
- Ranked-favored comfort picks: Reyna, Clove, Sage — forgiving kits that don't
  depend on teammates.
- Double-initiator comps are standard on most maps in coordinated play; solo
  queue succeeds more with self-sufficient agents.
- Viper remains mandatory-feeling on Icebox and Breeze whenever those maps are in
  the pool.
